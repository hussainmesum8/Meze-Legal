Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my recipes stored? Do I need an account?
There is no account. Your recipes, meal plans, shopping lists and kitchen are stored on your phone only. To keep a copy or move to a new phone, use Settings → Back up & restore to export a backup file, and import it on the new phone.
Which recipes can I import?
Links from cooking websites, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest and Facebook, as well as photos and cookbook pages. Share a link to Meze from any app, or paste it in Meze. Some posts do not contain a full recipe; if an import fails, email us the link and we will look at it.
How many recipes can I import for free?
Free users can import 5 recipes each week, from any mix of sources. Writing a recipe yourself and saving recipes from the built-in collection never count. Meze Premium gives unlimited imports.
What does Meze Premium include?
Unlimited imports, full nutrition details, building a whole week's meal plan, "Can cook now" and "Use these first" in your kitchen, sharing recipes as a designed PDF, the full recipe collection, shopping prices and trip totals, and unlimited week templates.
How do I cancel my subscription?
On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Meze. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions. You can also reach it from Meze's Settings. You keep Premium until the end of the period you have paid for.
I bought Premium but the app shows it locked. What should I do?
Open Meze's Settings and tap Restore purchases, while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you used to buy. If it still does not unlock, email us and we will help.
How accurate is the nutrition information?
Nutrition is estimated per serving from each ingredient's amount, using standard food composition data. It is a helpful guide, not a medical measurement. Always check labels if you have allergies or dietary needs.
How do I report a bug?
In Meze, open Settings → Report a bug. It opens an email already addressed to us, with your app and system version filled in. Tell us what happened and the steps to make it happen again.
