Common questions

Where are my recipes stored? On your phone only. There is no account. To move to a new phone, use Settings, Back up & restore. Backups include your recipe photos.

How many recipes can I import for free? 5 each week. Writing a recipe yourself does not count. Meze Pro gives unlimited imports.

A recipe would not import. Some posts do not include a full recipe. Email us the link and we will look at it.

I bought Meze Pro but it is locked. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel? On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Meze. On Android: Google Play, Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel.

Refunds? Purchases are made through Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them.

Privacy and AI reading. You can turn off AI recipe reading and usage reports in Settings. See our Privacy Policy, or email us to ask about your data.
