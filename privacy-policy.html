Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026  ·  Last Updated: October 15, 2026

Meze is built so that your kitchen stays yours. There is no account to create. Your recipes, meal plans, shopping lists and kitchen live on your phone, not on our servers. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

1. Who We Are

Meze ("we," "our," or "us") is a recipe, meal planning and grocery list app for iOS and Android, developed and operated by Mohammad Mesum Hussain Qureshi. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hussainmesum8@gmail.com.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Everything you create in Meze is stored locally on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your recipes, including recipes you import, write or save from the collection, and their photos;
  • Your meal plans, week templates, shopping lists, prices and aisle order;
  • Your kitchen (pantry) items and their dates;
  • Your favourites, cooking progress and app preferences, such as light or dark appearance.

This information is not sent to us. A backup file is created only when you choose to export one, and it goes wherever you choose to save or share it. Deleting the app deletes this information from your device.

3. Information That Leaves Your Device

Some features need the internet. Here is exactly what is sent, and why.

Recipe imports

When you import a recipe from a link (for example a cooking website, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest or Facebook), the link is sent to Meze's recipe reader, a server we operate in the European Union (Nuremberg, Germany). The reader opens the public page or post, reads the recipe, and returns it to your device. To turn a post or page into a recipe it may use the post's public caption, subtitles, audio and images, and it may use OpenAI's API to structure the recipe. OpenAI does not use data sent through its API to train its models. Recipes read from photos or cookbook pages are read on your device; no image is uploaded.

The reader keeps the finished recipe for a public link so the same link opens quickly next time. That copy is about the public post or page, not about you.

Free import allowance

Free users can import a set number of recipes each week. To count them fairly, the app sends a random install identifier created by the app (not your name, email or any device advertising identifier) with each import. The reader stores only a scrambled form of this identifier and the times of that week's imports, and deletes them once the week is over.

Ingredient pictures

When a recipe shows an ingredient picture, the app downloads it from the reader by the ingredient's name.

Purchases

Meze Premium is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription status. RevenueCat receives the app's random install identifier and your purchase records from the store.

Crash reports and usage statistics

To find and fix problems, Meze uses Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google. When the app crashes, a crash report is sent with technical details such as the app version, the device model, the operating system version and what the app was doing. To understand how the app is used, Meze sends usage events such as which screens are opened and when a recipe is imported, together with a random app instance identifier.

These reports never include your recipes, links, notes, names or any other content you create. Meze does not collect advertising identifiers, does not personalise ads, and does not use this data for advertising or tracking.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not ask for, or collect, your name, email address, phone number or any account credentials. There are no accounts.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos or files, except a photo or file you choose yourself, which stays on your device.
  • We do not collect payment card details.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Permissions

  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to scan a cookbook page or add a photo to a recipe. Images are processed on your device.
  • Notifications: used to tell you when a shared recipe is ready, and to remind you before a free trial ends. You can turn them off in your device's settings at any time.

6. How We Use Information

The limited information described above is used only to:

  • Import recipes you ask Meze to import;
  • Provide the free weekly import allowance and Meze Premium;
  • Find, diagnose and fix crashes and bugs;
  • Understand which features are used, so we can improve the app;
  • Reply to messages you send us.

8. Data Retention

  • Your recipes and other content stay on your device until you delete them or the app.
  • Import counts are deleted after one week.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Usage statistics are kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you.

9. Security

Data sent by the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your content stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by your device's own security. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information we handle.

10. Children's Privacy

Meze is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at hussainmesum8@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Your Rights and Choices

  • You can delete any recipe, plan or list in the app, or remove everything by deleting the app.
  • You can export your data at any time from Settings → Back up & restore.
  • You can turn off notifications in your device's settings.
  • You can manage or cancel Meze Premium in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because Meze does not hold accounts, the data we hold is limited to what is described above. To exercise any right, contact us at hussainmesum8@gmail.com.

12. International Transfers

Our recipe reader runs in the European Union. Our service providers may process data in other countries, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.
