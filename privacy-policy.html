Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Meze has no accounts. Your recipes, meal plans and shopping lists stay on your phone. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

1. Who we are

Meze is made by Mohammad Mesum Hussain Qureshi, Pakistan. Contact: hussainmesum8@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

Your recipes and their photos, meal plans, shopping lists, favourites and settings are stored only on your phone. We cannot see them. A backup file, including your recipe photos, is created only when you export one, and you choose where it goes. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. What leaves your phone

Importing a recipe from a link. Only after you allow AI recipe reading, Meze sends the link, and for a website the recipe text from the page, to its recipe reader, a server we run in Nuremberg, Germany. To write the recipe, the reader sends that text, or a post’s public caption, to OpenAI. When a caption is not enough, it also sends the post’s audio or a few pictures from it, and deletes them within minutes. The finished recipe is kept on the reader for up to 30 days, not linked to you, so the same link loads faster. You can turn AI recipe reading off in Settings; website recipes are then read on your phone only. Recipes from photos and cookbook pages are always read on your phone and are not uploaded.

Free imports. To count the 5 free imports each week, the app sends a random install code with each import. The reader keeps only a scrambled form of it, and deletes it after that week.

Ingredient pictures and notes. The app asks the reader for these by the ingredient’s name only.

Purchases. Apple or Google handle payment. We never see your card. RevenueCat confirms your subscription using the random install code and your purchase records.

Usage and crash reports. Google Firebase receives anonymous usage events (such as screens opened) and crash reports, with your device model, app version and approximate region, which Google works out from the connection. They never include your recipes, links or anything you write. You can turn them off in Settings with “Share usage and crash reports”.

Server logs. The reader keeps a short log of requests, with only part of your network address, for 7 days to stop abuse.

4. Who receives it

  • Hetzner (Germany), which hosts the reader.
  • OpenAI, which reads imported posts and pages. It does not use this data to train its models and keeps it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.
  • Google Firebase, for usage and crash reports.
  • RevenueCat, Apple and Google, for purchases.

We do not sell or share your data for advertising. Some of these companies process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own data protection terms.

5. How long it is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it or the app.
  • Recipes kept on the reader: up to 30 days. Import counts: one week. Server logs: 7 days.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days. Usage events: up to 14 months.
  • Emails you send us: only as long as needed to help you.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off AI recipe reading or usage and crash reports in Meze’s Settings at any time.
  • Delete any recipe in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on your phone.
  • Export your data from Settings, Back up & restore.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because there are no accounts, we hold very little. To make a request, email hussainmesum8@gmail.com.

7. Children

Meze is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Questions: hussainmesum8@gmail.com.
